# Ascend Lending Partners — Instagram Brand Rules

Source of truth: live site (`ascend-lending/styles.css`), not the generic palette from the
original ChatGPT brief. Colours and fonts below override anything else.

## Colours
See `colours.json`. Primary: Navy `#0A1A2F`, Gold `#C8A451`, Off-white `#F7F8FA`.

## Typography
- Headings: **Cinzel** (SemiBold/Bold) — premium editorial serif, matches site
- Body / supporting text: **Montserrat** (Regular/Medium/SemiBold)
- No decorative scripts, no condensed faces, minimum 30px effective body size at 1080x1350

## Canvas
- 1080 x 1350 px, PNG, one slide per file, never a grid/contact sheet as a final deliverable
- Safe margin: 90px on all sides for text; footer zone reserved in bottom 140px

## Every slide must include
- Ascend Lending Partners logotype (`Ascend-LP-Logo-Blue-Gold.png`)
- `ascendlendingpartners.com.au`
- Slide number in `n/5` format
- Consistent footer bar

## Backgrounds (test batch decision)
Python-generated only — no external image-generation API. Geometric / gradient panels in
brand navy and gold, using subtle architectural line motifs (angled panels, thin rules,
corner brackets) rather than photography. Avoids AI-hand artefacts, gibberish signage and
generic stock-photo clichés entirely.

## Banned phrases (never appear on a slide or in copy)
Guaranteed approval, guaranteed funding, everyone qualifies, no credit checks, cheapest rate,
best rate, instant money, free money, risk-free, no documents ever required, same-day funding
(unqualified), 100% approval.

## Disclaimers
- Long form (caption): "General information only. This does not constitute financial, legal,
  accounting or tax advice. Credit is subject to lender assessment, eligibility and approval
  criteria. Fees, charges, terms and conditions may apply."
- Short form (on-slide, slide 5 only): "General information only. Credit subject to lender
  assessment and approval criteria."

## Compliance notes carried over from site copy
The website states specific figures (e.g. "$750K limit", "up to 95% advance", "24hr
settlement"). These are used on slides as illustrative maximums with qualifying language
("up to", "may", "subject to assessment") — never presented as guaranteed outcomes for every
applicant.
