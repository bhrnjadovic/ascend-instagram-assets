Full doc and low doc aren't a hierarchy of 'proper' versus 'lesser' lending, they're two different ways of demonstrating the same thing: that your business can service the loan.

Full doc uses complete financials to unlock potentially sharper terms. Low doc accepts alternative evidence like bank statements or BAS lodgements for businesses whose income doesn't fit a standard template, but it always still requires evidence of income.
