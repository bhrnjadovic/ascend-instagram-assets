A credit file is essentially a track record — how you've managed credit in the past, including repayment history, defaults, and recent enquiries. For business lending, both the business's and the director's personal credit files can come into play, especially where a personal guarantee is involved.

It's worth reviewing your own file before applying so there are no surprises, and correcting anything inaccurate ahead of time.
