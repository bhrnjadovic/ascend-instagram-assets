Rather than researching and approaching lenders one at a time, a broker does that legwork for you — understanding your situation, then comparing it against a panel of lenders to find a genuine fit.

It's worth asking any broker how they're remunerated and what their process looks like, so you know exactly what you're getting from the relationship.
