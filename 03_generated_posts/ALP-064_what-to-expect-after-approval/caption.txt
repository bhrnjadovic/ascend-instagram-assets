Getting approved is a genuine milestone, but it's not quite the end of the process — there's usually a settlement stage involving final documentation, any outstanding conditions, and security registration where relevant.

Knowing this upfront helps you plan realistically around when funds will actually be available, rather than assuming approval and access happen on the same day.
