There's no universal green light for expansion, but there are useful signals: demand that's consistently outpacing capacity, cash flow that's already stable rather than stretched, and a clear picture of what the expansion will actually cost.

Expanding to solve an existing cash-flow problem rarely works — the better sequence is stabilising first, then expanding from a position of strength.
