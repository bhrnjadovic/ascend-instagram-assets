Fixed and variable rates each solve a different problem, and neither one is objectively superior.

A fixed rate offers certainty over the term, useful when you're budgeting tightly or expect rates to rise. A variable rate moves with the market, potentially rewarding you if rates fall, but with less predictability along the way.
