It's tempting to round up "just in case" or round down to keep repayments low, but both instincts can work against you. The better approach is costing the actual goal precisely, adding a sensible buffer, and checking that the resulting repayments genuinely fit your cash flow.

A lender's serviceability assessment will ultimately set the ceiling, but starting from a well-reasoned number puts you in a stronger position from the outset.
