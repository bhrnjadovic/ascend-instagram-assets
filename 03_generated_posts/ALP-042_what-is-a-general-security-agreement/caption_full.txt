Rather than securing a loan against one asset, a General Security Agreement gives the lender a registered interest across the company's assets as a whole. It's registered on the PPSR, which establishes the lender's priority relative to other creditors.

GSAs are a routine part of many business term loans, but it's worth understanding how one interacts with any other security already in place before agreeing to it.
