It's a genuinely common and confusing experience: the numbers say the business is profitable, yet the bank account tells a tighter story. The gap comes from timing, profit is recognised when a sale is made, not when the cash actually lands, and growth spending often happens well ahead of the return.

Recognising this distinction is what allows a genuinely healthy business to use short-term finance strategically, rather than seeing it as a sign of trouble.
