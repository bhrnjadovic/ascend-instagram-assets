A balloon or residual payment reduces what you pay along the way by pushing a portion of the asset's cost to a single payment at the end of the term. It can make monthly repayments more manageable, particularly for assets that hold their value well.

The trade-off is that the balloon amount still needs to be dealt with, paid out, refinanced, or covered by trading in the asset, so it's worth planning for that outcome from the start rather than leaving it to the end of the term.
