Neither reinvesting profit nor using finance is universally "better", they suit different situations. Reinvestment keeps things simple and debt-free but moves at the pace of what's already been earned; finance can move faster but comes with a cost and a repayment commitment.

The more useful approach is weighing each significant decision on its own merits, rather than defaulting to one path every time.
