If your business has a predictable quiet season, the cash-flow gap it creates is predictable too, which means it can be planned for rather than reacted to. Building a buffer during peak months and having a flexible facility on standby for the slower ones is often more effective than scrambling once trade drops off.

The key is arranging that flexibility ahead of the quiet period, not during it.
