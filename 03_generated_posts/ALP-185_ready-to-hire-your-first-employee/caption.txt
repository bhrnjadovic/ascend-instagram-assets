Taking on your first employee is a significant step, and the cash-flow timing can be one of the trickiest parts, wages start immediately, while the extra capacity they bring takes time to translate into more revenue.

Working capital finance can bridge that specific gap, so the decision to hire is driven by readiness, not by what happens to be sitting in the account that month.
