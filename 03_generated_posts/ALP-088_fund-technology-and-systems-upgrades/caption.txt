Outdated systems can quietly cost more in inefficiency than a well-timed upgrade would in finance repayments, but the upfront cost of hardware, software or a full platform change can still be a real barrier.

Financing the upgrade spreads that cost across the period you'll actually benefit from it, which is often a more sensible match than paying it all at once.
