Refinancing isn't about taking on more debt — it's about replacing what you already have with a facility that better suits where your business is now. That might mean a lower rate, a longer term, or consolidating several debts into one manageable repayment.

Before refinancing, it's worth weighing any exit costs on the existing facility against the new establishment fees and the actual savings on offer, so the total cost — not just the headline rate — guides the decision.
