It's natural to feel cautious about taking on debt, but treating “debt” as a single, uniform thing to avoid can mean missing out on genuinely sound decisions. Financing a specific, income-generating purpose is a very different proposition to borrowing to patch an ongoing shortfall.

The more useful question isn't “should I take on debt” in the abstract, but “does this specific facility, for this specific purpose, make sense.”
