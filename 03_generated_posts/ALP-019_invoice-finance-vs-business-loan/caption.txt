If your cash-flow gap comes from waiting on customers rather than needing new capital altogether, the right tool might not be a loan at all.

A business loan is new debt assessed against overall revenue. Invoice finance advances money against invoices you've already issued, typically scaling with your invoicing activity rather than requiring a fresh application each time.
