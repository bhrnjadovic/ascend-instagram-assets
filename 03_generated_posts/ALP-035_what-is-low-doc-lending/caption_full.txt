"Low doc" doesn't mean no assessment, it means the lender accepts a different form of evidence in place of full tax returns and financial statements, which suits many self-employed business owners whose paperwork doesn't tell the full story of current trading.

Bank statements, BAS lodgements or an accountant's letter are common substitutes. The trade-off is usually in pricing or facility size, so it's worth understanding both sides before applying.
