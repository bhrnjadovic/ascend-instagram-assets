Loan term is one of the biggest levers affecting how a facility feels day to day. A shorter term clears the debt faster and costs less in total interest, but the repayments are higher. A longer term eases monthly cash flow but adds to the total cost over time.

There's no universally "right" term — it depends on what the funds are being used for and how the business's cash flow is likely to look across that period.
