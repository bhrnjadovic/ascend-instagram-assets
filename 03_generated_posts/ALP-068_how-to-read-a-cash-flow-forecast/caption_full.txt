The value of a cash flow forecast isn't perfect accuracy — it's early warning. A well-maintained forecast shows roughly when a shortfall might occur, giving you weeks rather than days to plan around it, whether that's adjusting spending or arranging finance ahead of time.

Even a simple spreadsheet, updated regularly, beats no forecast at all.
