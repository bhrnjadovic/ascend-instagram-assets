Short-term finance genuinely helps when the issue is timing, a gap between a cost you've incurred and the revenue that will follow it. It's a much weaker fit when cash pressure is a symptom of a deeper, ongoing issue, like pricing that doesn't cover costs.

Being honest about which situation you're actually in leads to a better decision than reaching for finance as a default response to any cash pressure.
