Offering security doesn't just reduce lender risk, depending on the asset and lender, it can also open the door to larger facilities, longer terms, and potentially sharper pricing than an equivalent unsecured loan.

Property, equipment and other business assets can all be considered, with loan-to-value ratios and documentation requirements varying by lender and asset type.
