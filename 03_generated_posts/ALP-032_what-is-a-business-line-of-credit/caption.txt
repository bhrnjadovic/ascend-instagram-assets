Unlike a term loan, a line of credit isn't a single lump sum, it's an ongoing facility you draw on, repay, and draw on again as circumstances change.

That structure suits businesses whose funding needs shift throughout the year, rather than businesses with one clearly defined, one-off cost. Understanding how draw-downs and repayments interact with your limit is key to using the facility well.
