A cash-flow squeeze can feel alarming, but it's one of the most common challenges business owners face, and it doesn't necessarily reflect the underlying health of the business. Growth, seasonality and payment timing all create pressure that has nothing to do with profitability.

What matters is recognising the pattern early and having a plan, whether that's tightening collection terms or using short-term finance to bridge a gap.
