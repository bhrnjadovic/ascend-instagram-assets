Late payment is one of the most common, and most disruptive, cash-flow issues small businesses face. A single overdue invoice can delay your own supplier payments, and when several customers pay late at once, the effect compounds quickly.

Clear terms and consistent follow-up help, but they don't remove the timing risk entirely, which is where tools like invoice finance can reduce your reliance on customers paying exactly on time.
