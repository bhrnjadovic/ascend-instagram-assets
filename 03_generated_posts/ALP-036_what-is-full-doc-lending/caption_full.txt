Full doc lending is the more traditional path, tax returns, financial statements and supporting records go to the lender so they can form a complete view of your business's financial position.

It generally takes more preparation than a low doc application, but for established businesses with up-to-date records, it can open the door to larger facilities or more competitive pricing.
