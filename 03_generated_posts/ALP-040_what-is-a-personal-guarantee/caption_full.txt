A personal guarantee means that if the business can't meet its repayments, the director who signed the guarantee can be personally pursued for the debt. It's a standard feature of many business lending agreements, particularly for unsecured facilities or younger businesses without a long trading history.

Guarantees can be limited to a set amount or unlimited, so understanding exactly what you're agreeing to — and seeking independent legal advice if needed — matters before signing.
