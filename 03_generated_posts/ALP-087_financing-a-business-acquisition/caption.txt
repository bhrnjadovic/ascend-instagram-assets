Buying an existing business is a different scale of decision to everyday business lending, it involves understanding someone else's financials as closely as your own, and structuring finance around a purchase price rather than a single, well-defined need.

Because these deals typically take longer and involve more moving parts, starting the finance conversation early, well before a purchase agreement is signed, gives everyone more room to get the structure right.
