Transport and logistics businesses rely heavily on vehicles and equipment that carry significant upfront cost. Equipment finance is central here, letting operators grow or replace a fleet without paying full price outright, while working capital covers fuel and running costs.

Every industry has its own rhythm when it comes to cash flow and finance needs, understanding transport and logistics businesses's specific pattern is the first step to finding the right fit.

Vehicles are usually both the biggest asset and the biggest cost in transport, structuring finance around that reality matters.
