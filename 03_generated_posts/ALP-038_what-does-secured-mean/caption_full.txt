Security simply means a specific asset stands behind the loan, if the facility isn't repaid as agreed, the lender has recourse to that asset. In exchange for that reduced risk, secured lending can often support larger amounts or better pricing than an unsecured facility.

The right choice comes down to what assets you're willing to put forward and how that trade-off weighs against the terms on offer.
