The cash conversion cycle is a simple way to see where your money is tied up — how long stock sits before it sells, plus how long it takes customers to pay once it does. Businesses with long cycles often feel cash-flow pressure even while trading profitably.

Shortening either side of the cycle — faster stock turnover or faster collections — eases that pressure, and finance can help cover the gap while those improvements take hold.
