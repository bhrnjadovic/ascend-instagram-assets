Buying equipment outright feels simple, but simple isn't always the same as efficient.

Paying cash avoids finance costs entirely but locks up capital that could otherwise fund operations, stock, or growth. Equipment finance spreads the cost across the asset's productive life, a trade-off worth weighing against what else that capital could be doing.
