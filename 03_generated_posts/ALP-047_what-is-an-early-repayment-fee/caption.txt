If you're planning to pay off a business loan ahead of schedule — whether from a cash windfall or a refinance — it's worth checking whether an early repayment fee applies. These fees exist to cover the lender's loss of expected interest income, and they're more common on fixed-rate facilities.

Not every loan includes one, and some allow a certain amount of early repayment without charge, so reading the agreement — or asking directly — avoids surprises.
