A multi-year commitment can feel like a big step, especially for a specific, short-term need, which is exactly why short-term finance exists. Matching the facility's term to how long you'll actually need the funds, rather than defaulting to the longest available option, often fits better.

It's worth asking about the full range of term lengths before assuming a long commitment is the only path.
