The same handful of issues cause most delays: paperwork that's incomplete or out of date, a requested amount that doesn't line up with what the numbers can support, or unexplained activity sitting in bank statements.

None of these are complicated to avoid, they just take a bit of preparation and, ideally, a second set of eyes before the application goes in.
