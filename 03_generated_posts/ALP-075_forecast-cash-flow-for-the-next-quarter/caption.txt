You don't need sophisticated software to forecast the next quarter — a simple month-by-month list of expected income and expenses, built on realistic assumptions, is usually enough to reveal whether a shortfall is coming.

The real value comes from acting on what the forecast shows — deciding ahead of time how you'd respond if the lowest point looks tighter than you'd like.
