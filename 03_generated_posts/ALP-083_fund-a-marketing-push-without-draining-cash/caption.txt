A bigger marketing push often needs bigger upfront spend before the return shows up, which is exactly the kind of timing gap finance is suited to. Funding it separately, rather than draining working capital, keeps the rest of the business running normally while the campaign plays out.

The key is sizing the facility to a specific, considered campaign, not an open-ended spend.
