There's a tendency to assume finance is only worth the effort for large amounts — but plenty of genuine business needs are modest and specific, and the finance market has options built for exactly that. A line of credit, in particular, suits smaller, recurring needs without requiring you to borrow more than necessary.

The right move is comparing options for your actual amount, rather than assuming it's not worth pursuing.
