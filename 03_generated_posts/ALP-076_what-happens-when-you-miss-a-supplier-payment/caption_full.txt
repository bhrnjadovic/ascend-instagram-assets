A missed supplier payment rarely stays contained to that single invoice — it can affect the relationship, put future supply at risk, and sometimes lead to less favourable terms going forward.

If a payment is at risk of being missed, communicating with the supplier early tends to lead to a better outcome than going quiet — and short-term finance can sometimes prevent the situation altogether.
