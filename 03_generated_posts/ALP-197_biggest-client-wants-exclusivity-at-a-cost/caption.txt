An exclusivity offer from a major client is flattering and potentially very valuable, but it's worth treating the required investment — in capacity, staff or equipment — as seriously as the opportunity itself.

Finance can fund that commitment, letting you say yes to a significant opportunity without stretching the rest of the business too thin.
