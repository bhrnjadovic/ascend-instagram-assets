Retail and ecommerce businesses often need to fund stock well ahead of peak selling periods, with cash tied up in inventory until it sells through. A flexible line of credit or stock finance can smooth these cycles, particularly around major seasonal peaks.

Every industry has its own rhythm when it comes to cash flow and finance needs, understanding retail and ecommerce businesses's specific pattern is the first step to finding the right fit.

Whether online or in-store, retail cash flow follows the stock cycle, finance that flexes with that cycle tends to work best.
