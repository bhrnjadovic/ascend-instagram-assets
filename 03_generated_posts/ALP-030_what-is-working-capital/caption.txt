Working capital isn't the same as profit — it's the cash you have on hand to keep the lights on, stock shelves and staff paid while you wait for customers to pay you.

Many businesses are profitable on paper but still feel squeezed month to month because their working capital cycle is longer than their bill cycle. Recognising the difference is the first step toward managing it — whether that's tightening collection terms, adjusting stock levels, or bringing in short-term finance to bridge the gap.
