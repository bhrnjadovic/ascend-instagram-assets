The mechanics are simpler than they sound: you issue an invoice as normal, the lender advances a percentage of its value up front, and the remainder is paid to you (minus fees) once your customer settles the invoice.

It's a facility built around your debtor book rather than your assets, which makes it particularly relevant for businesses whose biggest cash-flow drag is simply waiting to get paid on trading terms.
