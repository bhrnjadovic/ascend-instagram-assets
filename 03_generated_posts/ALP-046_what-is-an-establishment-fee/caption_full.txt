An establishment fee covers the lender's cost of setting up your facility — assessing the application, preparing documentation and finalising the agreement. It's typically a one-off charge, separate from any ongoing fees or interest.

Because establishment fees vary between lenders and facility types, they're worth factoring into any comparison — a slightly lower rate can be offset by a higher upfront fee, so the total cost of the facility matters more than any single number.
