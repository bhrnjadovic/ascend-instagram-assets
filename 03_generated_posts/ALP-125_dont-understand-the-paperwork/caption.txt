Loan paperwork is genuinely dense, and it's completely reasonable to want it explained in plain language before signing. You're entitled to ask a broker or lender to walk through the rate, fees, repayment schedule and any security involved, in terms that actually make sense to you.

If someone seems reluctant to slow down and explain, that's worth noting, a good adviser wants you to understand exactly what you're agreeing to.
