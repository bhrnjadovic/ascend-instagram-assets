Interest-only repayments can look appealing on paper, but the trade-off is real: the principal doesn't reduce during that period, and it still needs to be repaid eventually.

Principal and interest repayments cost more per month but reduce your balance from day one. Interest-only can ease short-term cash flow, provided there's a clear plan for the principal once that period ends.
