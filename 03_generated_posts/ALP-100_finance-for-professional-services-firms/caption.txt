Professional services firms often bill in arrears — after work is delivered — creating a lag between cost and payment. A flexible line of credit can smooth staff and overhead costs while waiting on client payment cycles.

Every industry has its own rhythm when it comes to cash flow and finance needs — understanding professional services firms's specific pattern is the first step to finding the right fit.

Billing in arrears is standard in professional services — but it still creates a real cash-flow lag worth planning around.
