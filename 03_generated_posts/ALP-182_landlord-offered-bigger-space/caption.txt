An offer of more space from an existing landlord can feel like an easy yes, but it's worth treating the decision with the same rigour as any other expansion, costing the fit-out, the higher rent, and the timing properly before committing.

Once the numbers make sense, finance can fund the move without disrupting how the current space is running.
