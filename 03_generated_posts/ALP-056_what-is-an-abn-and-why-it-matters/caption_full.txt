Your ABN is more than a registration number — it's one of the first things a lender looks at to confirm your business is real, active, and trading. How long it's been registered often factors into which lenders and products you're eligible for.

If you've recently registered, that doesn't rule out finance — it just means certain lenders who specialise in newer businesses may be a better fit than others.
