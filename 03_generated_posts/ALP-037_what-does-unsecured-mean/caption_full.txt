"Unsecured" simply means no single asset is pledged against the loan — it doesn't mean there's no risk or no assessment involved. Lenders lean more heavily on trading history and cash flow, and a personal guarantee from directors is common.

The trade-off for that flexibility is usually a higher rate or a smaller facility size compared to secured lending, so it pays to weigh both paths against your business's situation.
