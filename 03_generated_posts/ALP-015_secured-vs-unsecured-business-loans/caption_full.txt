Secured or unsecured isn't a question of good versus bad — it's a question of what your business has, what it needs, and how those trade-offs line up for your situation.

Secured lending can unlock larger amounts and longer terms by using property or assets as collateral. Unsecured lending trades some of that capacity for speed and flexibility, assessed on revenue and trading history instead.
