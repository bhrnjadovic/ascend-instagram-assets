Behind the scenes, most lenders move through a similar sequence: verifying who you are and what your business is, checking credit history, and assessing whether the numbers support the amount requested.

Being ready to respond quickly if a lender asks for additional information is one of the simplest ways to keep an assessment moving rather than stalling.
