Landing a significant new contract is exciting, but delivering on it often means spending on staff, materials or equipment well before the first invoice is paid — a timing gap that can catch a business off guard if it isn't planned for.

Working capital finance, sized to the specific contract, can cover that delivery phase so the win doesn't become a cash-flow risk.
