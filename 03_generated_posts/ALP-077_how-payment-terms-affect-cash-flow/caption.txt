It's easy to focus on chasing overdue invoices, but the terms themselves are often the bigger lever, if you're offering 30-day terms while your own suppliers expect payment in seven, that gap exists regardless of how promptly customers pay within their terms.

Reviewing both sides of your payment terms is often the first, cheapest step before looking at finance to cover the gap.
