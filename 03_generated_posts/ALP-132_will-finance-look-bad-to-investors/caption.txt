It's a fair worry if you're answering to investors or business partners, but appropriately used finance is a completely normal part of running and growing a business. What tends to concern stakeholders isn't the existence of debt, it's debt that seems poorly considered or out of proportion to the business.

Being able to clearly explain the purpose, structure and repayment plan usually turns finance from a concern into a sign of considered management.
