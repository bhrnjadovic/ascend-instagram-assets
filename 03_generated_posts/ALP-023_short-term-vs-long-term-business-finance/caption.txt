Taking a long-term facility for a short-term need — or vice versa — is one of the more common structuring mistakes we see.

Short-term finance suits a defined, time-bound situation with a clear exit event. Long-term finance suits larger investments where spreading repayments over years makes more sense than compressing them into months.
