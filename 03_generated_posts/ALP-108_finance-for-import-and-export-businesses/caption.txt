Import and export businesses often pay suppliers well before goods arrive and sell, with shipping and customs adding further delay. Trade finance is designed specifically for this extended cycle, funding the gap between paying suppliers and receiving payment.

Every industry has its own rhythm when it comes to cash flow and finance needs — understanding import and export businesses's specific pattern is the first step to finding the right fit.

Long shipping and payment cycles are the defining cash-flow challenge for import and export businesses.
