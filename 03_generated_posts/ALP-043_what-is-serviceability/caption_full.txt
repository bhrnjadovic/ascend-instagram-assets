Serviceability answers one core question for a lender: after accounting for expenses and existing debt, can this business realistically afford the proposed repayments? It's assessed alongside security, not instead of it.

Because lenders often build in a buffer above current interest rates, and weigh seasonal or irregular income carefully, presenting clear and current financial information can make a real difference to how an application is assessed.
