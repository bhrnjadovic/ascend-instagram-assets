It's tempting to fund every opportunity at once, but growth that outpaces your systems, staff or serviceability tends to create its own problems, even when each individual decision looked sound on paper.

Pacing growth deliberately, and being selective about which opportunities to fund now versus later, generally leads to a more resilient outcome than growing as fast as finance alone would allow.
