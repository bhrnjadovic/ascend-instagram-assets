Being declined by a bank can feel like a dead end, but it usually reflects one institution's specific lending criteria rather than a verdict on your business. Non-bank and specialist lenders often assess risk differently, and many perfectly viable businesses simply don't fit a traditional bank's box.

Understanding why the bank said no — and matching that to a lender whose criteria fit better — is usually more productive than reapplying with the same bank.
