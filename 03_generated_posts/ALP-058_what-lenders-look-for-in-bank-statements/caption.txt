Bank statements are one of the most revealing documents in a loan application, they show, in real time, how the business actually operates rather than how it looks on paper. Lenders are typically scanning for consistency, signs of financial stress, and how existing commitments are being managed.

It's worth reviewing your own statements before applying, so you can explain any unusual activity upfront rather than leaving a lender to guess.
