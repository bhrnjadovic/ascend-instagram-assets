A broker's panel — the range of lenders they can actually compare on your behalf — is one of the more overlooked but important factors in choosing who to work with. A wider, more varied panel generally improves the odds of finding a facility that genuinely fits.

It's a completely fair question to ask upfront, and a transparent broker will answer it plainly.
