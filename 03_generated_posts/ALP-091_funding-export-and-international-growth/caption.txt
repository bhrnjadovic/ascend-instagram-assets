Exporting adds a layer of timing complexity that domestic trade usually doesn't — longer shipping times, international payment cycles, and sometimes currency considerations on top. Trade finance exists specifically to fund that gap, from production through to when an overseas customer actually pays.

Working with a lender who understands these cycles can make the difference between export growth being smooth or straining the business.
