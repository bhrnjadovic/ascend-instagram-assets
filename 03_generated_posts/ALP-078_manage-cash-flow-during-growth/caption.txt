Growth is one of the most common — and most avoidable — causes of a sudden cash squeeze, simply because new costs usually land well before the new revenue they're meant to generate does.

Forecasting that gap specifically, and arranging finance to fund it deliberately ahead of time, turns a growth phase into a planned investment rather than a scramble.
