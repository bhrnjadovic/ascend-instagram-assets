Lenders are trying to build an accurate, current picture of your business — the easier you make that, the faster and smoother assessment tends to go. Reconciled accounts, current BAS lodgements, and a clean separation between business and personal spending all help.

If your books have fallen behind, it's worth investing a little time (or your accountant's time) tidying them up before you apply, rather than after.
