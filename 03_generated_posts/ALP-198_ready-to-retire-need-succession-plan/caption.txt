Succession planning is as much about timing as it is about finance — starting the process well before the intended exit date generally supports a smoother transition and a stronger outcome, whether the business is passing to family, staff or an external buyer.

Finance is often part of the picture on the buyer's side, and it's worth understanding those options early in the process.
