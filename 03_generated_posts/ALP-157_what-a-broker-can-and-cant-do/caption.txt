Setting realistic expectations upfront leads to a far better experience — a broker's role is comparison, guidance and advocacy, but final approval always rests with the lender, and a broker isn't a substitute for independent financial, legal or tax advice.

A good broker is upfront about these boundaries rather than overselling what they can promise.
