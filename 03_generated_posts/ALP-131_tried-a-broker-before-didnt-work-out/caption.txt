A poor past experience with a broker is a completely valid reason for hesitation, and it's worth naming rather than glossing over. The reality is broker quality varies significantly, clear communication, honest trade-off explanations, and genuine follow-up should be the standard, not the exception.

If your last experience fell short of that, it's reasonable to expect better, and worth giving the process another chance with clearer expectations set upfront.
