Adding a new loan on top of existing ones isn't always the answer — sometimes restructuring what's already there does more for your business.

Refinancing replaces existing debt with new terms, potentially consolidating multiple facilities into one simplified repayment. An additional loan keeps everything separate, which can suit a genuinely new and distinct need but adds another facility to manage.
