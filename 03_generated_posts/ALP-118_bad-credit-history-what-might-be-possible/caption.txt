A less-than-perfect credit history is more common than it might feel, and it doesn't automatically close every door — though it does typically mean navigating toward lenders who specialise in more complex situations, often with different terms than a standard facility.

Being upfront about your history from the outset, rather than hoping it goes unnoticed, tends to lead to a more realistic and productive conversation.
