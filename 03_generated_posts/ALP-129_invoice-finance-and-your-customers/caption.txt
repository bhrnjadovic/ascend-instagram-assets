It's a common and understandable concern — nobody wants customers to feel like the business is under financial pressure. In practice, invoice finance can often be arranged without customers being aware at all, and where notice is required, it's typically a standard administrative step rather than anything that changes how the relationship feels.

It's worth raising this preference upfront, since structures and disclosure norms vary between lenders.
