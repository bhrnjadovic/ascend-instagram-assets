Agricultural businesses often earn income in concentrated seasonal windows, while costs like feed, fuel and wages continue year-round. Working capital and equipment finance are widely used to bridge these cycles and fund machinery.

Every industry has its own rhythm when it comes to cash flow and finance needs, understanding agriculture and farming businesses's specific pattern is the first step to finding the right fit.

Seasonal income against year-round costs is one of the oldest cash-flow challenges in business, agriculture has its own tools to manage it.
