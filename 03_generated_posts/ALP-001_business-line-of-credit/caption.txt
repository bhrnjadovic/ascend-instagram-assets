A business line of credit isn't a lump-sum loan — it's a standing facility you can draw from, repay, and redraw as your business needs change. For businesses with seasonal cycles or unpredictable cash-flow timing, that flexibility can matter more than a one-off loan amount.

Interest is typically only charged on funds actually drawn, and approved limits can support both secured and unsecured structures depending on your circumstances.
