Security is simply the asset a lender can claim if a loan isn't repaid, it's what makes a secured facility different from an unsecured one, and it's a key factor in the terms a lender will offer.

Understanding what's being offered as security, and what happens if repayments fall behind, is essential before entering any secured lending arrangement.
