Revenue on paper and cash in the bank are two different things. Cash flow measures the actual timing of money moving in and out of your business — and it's timing, not total revenue, that determines whether you can pay this week's wages or next month's rent.

Understanding your cash flow cycle — how long it takes to get paid versus how quickly your own bills fall due — helps you spot pressure points early and plan around them, rather than reacting under stress.
