New equipment usually means one of two choices: pay cash and tie up capital, or finance it and keep that capital working elsewhere.

Equipment finance can cover vehicles, machinery, medical equipment and technology, structured as a chattel mortgage, hire purchase or lease depending on how you plan to use and eventually own the asset. New and used equipment can both be considered, and terms are generally matched to how long the asset will remain productive.
