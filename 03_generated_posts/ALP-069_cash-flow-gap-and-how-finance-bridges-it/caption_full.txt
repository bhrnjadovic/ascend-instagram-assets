A cash flow gap is purely about timing — the days or weeks where bills fall due before customer payments land. It's different to a business that's structurally losing money, and it's exactly what short-term finance is designed to address.

The key is being clear-eyed about which situation you're actually in, since a timing gap and an ongoing shortfall call for very different responses.
