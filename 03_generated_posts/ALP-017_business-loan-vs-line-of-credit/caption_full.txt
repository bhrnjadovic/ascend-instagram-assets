Not every funding need looks the same, and the right structure depends on the shape of that need.

A business loan gives you a fixed amount for a defined purpose, repaid on a set schedule. A line of credit gives you standing access to draw from, repay and redraw — useful when your capital needs move around rather than sitting still.
