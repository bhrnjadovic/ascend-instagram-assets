Most business owners default to their existing bank, without realising there's a much broader market of non-bank commercial lenders assessing businesses differently.

Banks often suit well-established businesses with complete financials and time to spare. Non-bank lenders can move faster and assess more flexibly, though the market varies widely lender to lender.
