It's a legitimate concern, credit applications can leave a mark on your file, and applying to several lenders in quick succession can compound that effect. The better approach is comparing options first, then applying to the lender genuinely most likely to fit, rather than casting a wide net.

This is one of the practical reasons a broker can help, doing the comparison work upfront, so the formal application happens once, with the right lender.
