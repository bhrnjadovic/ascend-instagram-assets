It's easy to fixate on a specific product name, but the better starting point is the problem you're actually solving. A short-term cash gap, a new piece of equipment, and a growth opportunity all call for different structures — and sometimes a combination.

A broker's role is largely this: translating your actual need into the right type of facility, rather than fitting your situation to whatever product is top of mind.
