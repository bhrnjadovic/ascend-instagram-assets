Rather than paying cash for a vehicle, machine or piece of technology, equipment finance spreads the cost over time, with the asset itself typically standing as security for the facility.

Structures vary: some agreements are set up so you fully own the asset at the end, others include a balloon or residual payment to lower regular repayments. The right structure depends on how the asset will be used and how long you plan to keep it.
