Applying for business finance can feel opaque if you don't know what's coming next. In practice, it follows a fairly consistent sequence: understanding your needs, gathering documentation, matching to suitable lenders, submitting, and working through assessment.

Knowing this sequence upfront, and roughly how long each stage takes, makes the whole process considerably less stressful.
