A line of credit and a business credit card can feel similar, both revolve, both flex with use, but they're generally built for different scales of spending.

A line of credit tends to suit larger, less frequent draws, while a credit card is built for everyday transactions with short interest-free windows. Plenty of businesses use both, for different purposes.
