A credit limit sets the maximum balance you can draw against a revolving facility at any point in time — it's set by the lender based on their assessment of your business, not simply the amount you ask for.

Because the limit itself stays fixed while your available balance moves as you draw down and repay, understanding the difference between the two helps you plan how much headroom you actually have at any given time.
