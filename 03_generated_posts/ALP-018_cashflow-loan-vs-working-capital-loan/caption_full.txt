These two products get used interchangeably in conversation, but they're built for different situations.

A cashflow loan addresses a specific, short-term timing gap, expenses landing before revenue does. A working capital loan is built for the ongoing, ordinary running of the business, often with more flexible structures for needs that don't have a single end date.
