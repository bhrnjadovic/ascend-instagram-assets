Franchise businesses often have relatively predictable set-up and ongoing costs defined by the franchise agreement, which can make finance planning more structured than for an independent business. Lenders may also factor in the franchisor's track record.

Every industry has its own rhythm when it comes to cash flow and finance needs — understanding franchise businesses's specific pattern is the first step to finding the right fit.
