Waiting 30, 60 or 90 days to get paid doesn't mean your cash flow has to wait too.

Invoice finance lets eligible B2B businesses access a percentage of an unpaid invoice's value ahead of the customer's payment date. It isn't a new loan — it's an advance against revenue you've already earned, and facilities can typically scale automatically as your invoicing grows.

It suits businesses that invoice other businesses on trade terms, with a reasonably consistent invoicing pattern and creditworthy customers.
