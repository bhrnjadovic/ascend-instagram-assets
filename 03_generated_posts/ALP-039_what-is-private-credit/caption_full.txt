Private credit sits outside the traditional bank system — it's finance provided by private funds and specialist non-bank lenders, who often apply different assessment criteria and can move more quickly than a conventional bank process.

It's become a significant part of how Australian businesses access finance, particularly where bank lending doesn't fit the business's situation or timeline.
